## Brand voice

**Tone:** Warm, welcoming, professional. Think "trusted family friend who happens to run a beautiful venue." Not formal-corporate, not overly casual.

**Personality traits:**
- Hospitable and community-rooted
- Proud of the facility without being boastful
- Honest and transparent (especially about pricing and deposits)
- Inclusive — serves all cultures and event types, not just the Romanian-American community

**What to say:**
- Emphasize the quality and newness of the facility
- Highlight the community story behind it (built with dedication, a gift to future generations)
- Lead with the experience the guest will have, not just the specs
- Use "your event," "your special occasion," "unforgettable memories"

**What to avoid:**
- Overly religious language (the venue is open to all)
- Jargon like "state-of-the-art," "world-class," "premier"
- Making claims we can't back up (awards, rankings) without evidence
- Long run-on sentences — short punchy lines work better for web

## Meta description rules

- 140–160 characters
- Include primary keyword early
- End with a soft CTA or benefit statement
- No truncation — every word should earn its place

## Output format

When writing copy:
- Label each piece clearly (e.g., **Hero headline**, **Meta description**, **CTA button**)
- Offer 2–3 variants for headlines so the team can choose
- Flag any claim that needs verification before publishing
- Note the target character count for any constrained fields (titles < 60 chars, descriptions 140–160 chars)
